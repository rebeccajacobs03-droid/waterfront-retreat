The property contains waterfront hazards.

You assume all risks associated with your stay.

In consideration of being permitted to stay at Waterfront Retreat LLC, use the property, amenities, facilities, recreational areas, and participate in any activities available on the property, I hereby agree on behalf of myself, my heirs, assigns, personal representatives, estate, and any member of my party to the terms of this Guest Agreement, Release of Liability, Assumption of Risk, and Waiver.

AGREEMENT TO FOLLOW RULES

I agree to observe and obey all posted rules, safety notices, warnings, and instructions provided by Waterfront Retreat LLC, Hitch LLC, and their owners, employees, representatives, or agents. I further agree to follow any oral instructions or directions provided by management, employees, representatives, or agents.

ADULTS-ONLY PROPERTY

I understand that Waterfront Retreat LLC is an adults-only property. Due to unrestricted lake access, docks, hot tubs, fire pits, stairs, elevated decks, uneven terrain, and other waterfront hazards, children and infants are not permitted on the property at any time.

I agree to comply with this policy and accept full responsibility for any violation by me or any member of my party.

RESPONSIBILITY FOR DAMAGES

I understand that I am responsible for any damage to cabins, accommodations, furnishings, amenities, equipment, property, excessive cleaning, missing items, unauthorized guests, or policy violations caused by me or any member of my party.

I agree that any charges for damages, excessive cleaning, missing property, unauthorized guests, policy violations, or other amounts owed under this agreement shall be due immediately upon demand and payable within thirty (30) days of written notice from Waterfront Retreat LLC.

PROPERTY POLICIES

I understand and agree that Waterfront Retreat LLC is a non-smoking property. Smoking or vaping inside any cabin may result in additional cleaning fees, damage charges, and removal from the property without refund.

I understand that only registered guests are permitted on the property unless prior written approval has been obtained from management. Unauthorized visitors may result in additional fees, cancellation of the reservation, or removal from the property without refund.

NATURAL ENVIRONMENT ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Waterfront Retreat LLC is located in a natural waterfront setting. Guests may encounter insects, spiders, mosquitoes, bees, wasps, ants, snakes, birds, fish, waterfowl, wildlife, algae, aquatic vegetation, pollen, mud, leaves, weather conditions, and other naturally occurring environmental conditions commonly associated with outdoor and waterfront properties.

While reasonable efforts are made to maintain the property and accommodations, Waterfront Retreat LLC cannot guarantee that insects, wildlife, or other natural environmental conditions will not be present on the property or occasionally enter cabins, structures, or guest accommodations.

Guests acknowledge and accept these conditions as an inherent part of the property's natural environment and assume all risks associated therewith.

Waterfront Retreat LLC, Hitch LLC, and the Releasees shall not be liable for any injury, illness, allergic reaction, property damage, inconvenience, loss, or other damages arising from insects, wildlife, weather, vegetation, natural terrain, or other naturally occurring environmental conditions.

The presence of insects, wildlife, weather conditions, or other natural environmental factors, whether outdoors or within guest accommodations, shall not constitute grounds for a refund, discount, cancellation, relocation, or reimbursement of any kind.

PETS, EMOTIONAL SUPPORT ANIMALS, AND SERVICE ANIMALS

Waterfront Retreat LLC maintains a no-pets policy. Pets, emotional support animals, comfort animals, therapy animals, and companion animals are not permitted unless prior written authorization is provided by management.

Service animals are accommodated as required by applicable law. Guests with service animals are responsible for keeping the animal under control at all times and for any damage, injury, cleaning costs, or disturbances caused by the animal.

Unauthorized animals may result in cleaning fees, damage charges, cancellation of the reservation, removal from the property without refund, or other remedies permitted by law.

ALCOHOL, DRUGS, AND ILLEGAL ACTIVITY

Alcohol may be consumed only by guests of legal drinking age and in compliance with all applicable laws.

Guests shall not operate any boat, watercraft, vehicle, recreational equipment, or engage in any activity while impaired by alcohol, illegal drugs, controlled substances, or any other intoxicating substance.

Illegal drugs and unlawful activities are strictly prohibited on the property.

Guests acknowledge that alcohol and intoxicating substances increase the risks associated with waterfront activities, swimming, docks, hot tubs, fire pits, stairs, decks, and other amenities, and voluntarily assume all related risks.

Illegal activity, disorderly conduct, public intoxication, excessive noise, harassment, threats, violence, property damage, or any behavior that creates a safety risk may result in immediate removal from the property without refund and may be reported to law enforcement.

FIREARMS, WEAPONS, FIREWORKS, AND HAZARDOUS ACTIVITIES

Guests must comply with all applicable laws regarding firearms, weapons, fireworks, explosives, and hazardous materials.

The discharge of any firearm, air gun, bow, crossbow, fireworks, explosive device, or similar item on the property is strictly prohibited.

Guests shall not engage in unsafe, reckless, threatening, unlawful, or disruptive conduct involving firearms, weapons, fireworks, explosives, or hazardous materials.

Any violation of this policy may result in immediate removal from the property without refund and may be reported to law enforcement.

RIGHT TO REMOVE GUESTS

Waterfront Retreat LLC reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to refuse service, terminate a guest's stay, remove any guest or visitor from the property, and/or contact law enforcement if any guest or member of a guest's party violates this agreement, engages in illegal activity, creates a safety hazard, disturbs other guests, damages property, or otherwise fails to comply with property rules and policies.

In such circumstances, the guest may be required to leave the property immediately and may forfeit any refund, reimbursement, or compensation for unused accommodations, subject to applicable law.
